Services

Our Professional Physiotherapy Services

Comprehensive assessment, tailored treatment plans, and evidence-based rehabilitation.

Orthopedic Physiotherapy

Diagnosis and treatment for bones, joints, ligaments, and muscles—restoring mobility, reducing pain, and strengthening after injuries or surgery.

Sports Physiotherapy

Prevention, assessment, and rehab of sports injuries. Return to play faster and safer with sport-specific conditioning and training programs.

Neurological Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation for stroke, Parkinson’s, spinal cord injury, and MS—improving mobility, balance, coordination, and independence.

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Care for infants, children, and teens to support healthy growth—improving posture, strength, motor skills, and functional movement.

Chest Physiotherapy

Airway clearance and breathing techniques for asthma, bronchitis, pneumonia, and COPD to enhance lung function and comfort.

Home Care

Personalized physiotherapy at home for elderly, post-surgical, or mobility-limited patients—ensuring continuity and convenience.
