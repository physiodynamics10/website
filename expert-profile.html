Founder & Chief Physiotherapist

Dr. Sandra Thomas (PT)

UB City Centre Building, Near Crescent Public School, Panamaram, Wayanad, Kerala

Dr. Sandra Thomas (PT) is the Founder and Chief Physiotherapist at Physio Dynamics, Wayanad's dedicated physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation centre in Panamaram. With over 4 years of hands-on clinical experience, she delivers evidence-based, results-driven physiotherapy across musculoskeletal, neurological, geriatric, and sports rehabilitation.

She independently manages the complete patient journey from accurate clinical assessment and diagnosis to treatment planning, rehabilitation protocols, and discharge with a strong focus on long-term recovery rather than temporary relief.

As an HCPC (UK) Registered Physiotherapist (ID: PH142734) with a FIFA Diploma in Football Medicine and Life Membership in the IAP, she maintains international clinical standards in every treatment protocol. Sandra believes informed patients recover better — patient education is central to every session at Physio Dynamics.
