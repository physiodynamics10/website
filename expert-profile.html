Founder & Chief Physiotherapist

Sandra Thomas, BPT

Physio Dynamics, Panamaram, Wayanad, Kerala

Sandra Thomas is the Founder and Chief Physiotherapist at Physio Dynamics — Wayanad's dedicated physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation centre in Panamaram. With over 4 years of hands-on clinical experience, she delivers evidence-based, results-driven physiotherapy across musculoskeletal, neurological, geriatric, and sports rehabilitation.

She independently manages the complete patient journey — from accurate clinical assessment and diagnosis to treatment planning, rehabilitation protocols, and discharge — with a strong focus on long-term recovery rather than temporary relief.

As an HCPC (UK) Registered Physiotherapist (ID: PH142734) and a Life Member of the Indian Association of Physiotherapists (IAP), she maintains international clinical standards in every treatment protocol. Sandra believes informed patients recover better — patient education is central to every session at Physio Dynamics.
