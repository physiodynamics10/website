Chief Physiotherapist

Sandra Thomas, BPT

Sandra Thomas is the Founder and Chief Physiotherapist at Physio Dynamics. With over 4+ years of clinical experience, she delivers evidence-based rehabilitation across musculoskeletal, neurological, and sports physiotherapy.

She independently manages the complete patient journey — from clinical assessment and diagnosis to personalized treatment planning — with a core focus on long-term recovery and patient education.

As an HCPC (UK) Registered professional and a life member of the Indian Association of Physiotherapists, she maintains international standards in every treatment protocol.
