What Is Orthopedic Physiotherapy?

Orthopedic physiotherapy (also called musculoskeletal physiotherapy) is a specialised branch of physical therapy focused on the assessment, diagnosis, and evidence-based treatment of disorders affecting the bones, joints, muscles, tendons, and ligaments. It is the most widely indicated form of physiotherapy for pain conditions in India and globally.

At Physio Dynamics, located in UB City Centre Building near Crescent Public School, Panamaram, Wayanad, every orthopedic physiotherapy session is conducted one-on-one by Sandra Thomas — a BPT-qualified, HCPC-registered physiotherapist (UK). This means you receive the undivided attention and clinical expertise of a specialist during every visit, rather than being delegated to an assistant.

Our evidence-based protocols combine hands-on manual therapy with targeted exercise prescription, electrotherapy modalities (TENS, ultrasound, dry needling), and biomechanical retraining — designed not only to eliminate pain, but to restore joint mobility, rebuild strength, and prevent future recurrence.

Conditions We Treat

Orthopedic physiotherapy is appropriate for anyone experiencing acute or chronic joint pain, stiffness, muscle weakness, or restricted movement. We regularly treat:

  • Knee pain (osteoarthritis, meniscus tears, runner's knee)
  • Lower back pain & lumbar disc herniation (slip disc)
  • Sciatica — radiating leg pain & nerve compression
  • Neck pain & cervical spondylosis
  • Shoulder pain (frozen shoulder, rotator cuff tears)
  • Hip arthritis & labral pathologies
  • Post-fracture rehabilitation & joint mobilization
  • Post-surgical rehab (knee replacement, ACL reconstruction)
  • Arthritis management (osteoarthritis & rheumatoid)
  • Tendon & ligament sprains (Achilles, ankle, elbow)
  • Plantar fasciitis & heel/foot pain
  • Postural syndromes & occupational stiffness

Patients across Panamaram, Mananthavady, Sulthan Bathery, and Kalpetta trust Physio Dynamics for dedicated, 1-on-1 orthopedic rehabilitation.

Our Orthopedic Rehabilitation Approach

Manual Therapy & Mobilisation

Specialised joint mobilization, passive stretching, and soft tissue myofascial release to restore physiological joint glide, reduce muscular guarding, and relieve acute stiffness.

Therapeutic Exercise & Loading

Targeted strengthening and progressive resistance regimens to offload painful joints, correct muscle imbalances, and rebuild functional endurance.

Advanced Pain Relief Modalities

Clinical dry needling, therapeutic ultrasound, and TENS electrotherapy to rapidly manage localized inflammation, spasm, and deep neuropathic discomfort.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Evidence-based, phased rehabilitation protocols following ACL reconstruction, total knee/hip replacement, or spinal surgery to safely return to full mobility.

What to Expect During Your Orthopedic Treatment

  1. Comprehensive Diagnostic Assessment (45–60 min): Detailed physical examination covering joint range of motion, muscle strength grading, special orthopedic tests, posture analysis, and functional movement screening.
  2. Personalized Recovery Blueprint: Sandra Thomas discusses your clinical diagnosis, root cause of pain, expected recovery timeline, and tailor-made treatment milestones.
  3. One-on-One Hands-On Treatment (30–45 min): Active physical therapy sessions combining manual joint mobilisations, guided corrective exercises, and advanced pain modalities.
  4. Home Exercise & Ergonomic Coaching: Customized home exercise program and practical workplace posture advice to maintain gains between sessions.
  5. Outcome Reassessment & Relapse Prevention: Regular progress evaluations every 4 sessions with progression to functional loading and long-term joint health maintenance.
