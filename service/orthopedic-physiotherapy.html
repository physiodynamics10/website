About Orthopedic Physiotherapy

Orthopedic physiotherapy focuses on the diagnosis, management, and treatment of disorders of the musculoskeletal system. This includes conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and tendons.

Our orthopedic physiotherapy services in Wayanad are designed to help patients recover from injuries, manage chronic conditions, and improve overall physical function through evidence-based treatment approaches.

Common Conditions We Treat

  • Back and neck pain
  • Arthritis (Osteoarthritis & Rheumatoid)
  • Joint pain (knee, shoulder, hip)
  • Sports injuries
  • Tendonitis and bursitis
  • Fracture rehabilitation
  • Post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Sprains and strains
  • Muscle tears
  • Postural problems

What to Expect During Your Visit

  1. Comprehensive Assessment: Thorough evaluation of your condition, medical history, and functional limitations
  2. Diagnosis: Clear explanation of your condition and treatment options
  3. Personalized Treatment Plan: Customized therapy plan based on your specific needs and goals
  4. Hands-on Treatment: Manual therapy and guided exercises during sessions
  5. Home Program: Exercises and advice for continued progress at home
  6. Progress Tracking: Regular assessment of improvement and plan adjustments

Benefits of Treatment

  • Pain reduction
  • Improved mobility
  • Increased strength
  • Faster recovery
  • Injury prevention
  • Better posture
  • Enhanced quality of life
