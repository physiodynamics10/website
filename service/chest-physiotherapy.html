About Chest Physiotherapy

Chest physiotherapy, also known as respiratory physiotherapy, is a specialized branch that focuses on the assessment and treatment of patients with respiratory disorders. Our techniques are designed to help clear mucus from the lungs, improve breathing patterns, and enhance overall lung function.

At Physio Dynamics Wayanad, our certified respiratory physiotherapists provide evidence-based interventions to help patients breathe easier, reduce hospital stays, and improve quality of life for those with acute or chronic respiratory conditions.

Conditions We Treat

  • Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
  • Asthma and Bronchitis
  • Pneumonia and Chest Infections
  • Cystic Fibrosis
  • Bronchiectasis
  • Post-operative Respiratory Complications
  • Neuromuscular Respiratory Weakness
  • Interstitial Lung Disease
  • Post-COVID Respiratory Rehabilitation
  • Respiratory Muscle Weakness

Our Treatment Techniques

Breathing Exercises

Techniques like pursed-lip breathing, diaphragmatic breathing, and segmental breathing to improve respiratory muscle function.

Manual Techniques

Chest percussion, vibration, and shaking to help loosen and clear mucus from the airways.

Respiratory Muscle Training

Specialized exercises and devices to strengthen breathing muscles and improve endurance.

Activity Pacing & Exercise

Gradual conditioning programs to improve exercise tolerance and reduce breathlessness.


Specialized Programs

Post-COVID Rehabilitation

Specialized program for patients recovering from COVID-19 with persistent respiratory symptoms.

Pre & Post Surgical Care

Respiratory preparation before surgery and recovery support after thoracic or abdominal surgery.

Home Care Program

Mobile respiratory care for patients who cannot travel to the clinic.

Smoking Cessation Support

Comprehensive program to help quit smoking and improve lung health.

Benefits of Treatment

  • Improved breathing capacity
  • Reduced breathlessness
  • Better mucus clearance
  • Increased exercise tolerance
  • Reduced hospital admissions
  • Enhanced quality of life
  • Better sleep quality
  • Improved energy levels

Who Can Benefit?

  • COPD patients
  • Asthma sufferers
  • Post-surgical patients
  • Neurological patients
  • Elderly with respiratory issues
  • Post-COVID recovery patients
