Why Sports Physiotherapy?

More than pain relief

Sports physiotherapy combines injury management with movement, strength and sport-specific rehabilitation.

Built around your sport and your goal

Sports physiotherapy focuses on preventing, assessing and treating injuries related to sports and exercise. It can support recreational players as well as competitive athletes.

At Physio Dynamics, rehabilitation is progressed according to your injury, movement needs and return-to-sport goals.

  • Injury assessment and movement screening
  • Strength, mobility and conditioning
  • Sport-specific rehabilitation
  • Injury prevention strategies
  • Structured return-to-sport progression

Our Approach

A clear path back to your sport

Each stage builds on the previous one so rehabilitation can move from basic recovery to sport-specific function.

01 / ASSESS

Injury Assessment

Functional movement, biomechanical and sport-specific testing to understand the problem and contributing factors.

02 / REBUILD

Strength & Conditioning

Progressive strength, mobility, flexibility and conditioning based on your current capacity.

03 / SPECIFIC

Sport-Specific Rehab

Exercises and drills are progressed toward the movement and physical demands of your sport.

04 / RETURN

Return-to-Sport

A structured progression using functional criteria before returning to training and competition.
