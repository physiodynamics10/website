Advanced Neuro Care

Specialized recovery for the nervous system

Neurological physiotherapy addresses physical impairments caused by conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves. Through principles of neuroplasticity, targeted therapeutic exercises encourage the central nervous system to form new neural connections.

Whether regaining limb function after a stroke, managing progressive tremors in Parkinson's, or recovering from facial nerve palsy, our evidence-based protocols help patients regain maximum functional independence.

Why specialized neurological rehab?

  • Individualized motor control and tone management
  • Task-specific functional repetition for neuroplastic recovery
  • Advanced balance, postural stability, and gait re-education
  • Spasticity reduction and contracture prevention
  • Dedicated home visit service across Wayanad for bedridden patients

Our Approach

The 4-stage neuro-recovery roadmap

A structured, compassionate pathway from initial impairment to functional freedom.

STAGE 01

Neuro Assessment

In-depth clinical evaluation of muscle tone, reflexes, sensory function, and balance.

STAGE 02

Neuro-Facilitation

Inhibition of abnormal muscle tone and facilitation of dormant motor patterns.

STAGE 03

Task-Specific Retraining

Repetitive functional training: transfers, standing balance, and walking with aids.

STAGE 04

Home Independence

Caregiver education, home safety adaptations, and long-term maintenance programs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Neurological physiotherapy FAQs

Answers to common questions regarding neurorehabilitation and stroke recovery.

Most patients notice improvements in balance, gait speed, and mobility within 4 to 6 weeks of consistent neurological physiotherapy sessions. At Physio Dynamics in Wayanad, therapy focuses on movement cues, posture correction, and fall prevention to maximize independence.

Neurological physiotherapy is a specialized rehabilitation program for individuals with conditions affecting the nervous system (brain, spinal cord, nerves), such as stroke, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, or brain injuries. It aims to improve movement, strength, balance, and independence.

Neurorehabilitation helps the brain reorganize and create new pathways (neuroplasticity). Through targeted exercises, gait training, and repetitive functional tasks, survivors can relearn movements, regain strength, and recover lost daily functions.

Yes, Physio Dynamics provides dedicated home care physiotherapy across Wayanad for stroke and neurological patients who have limited mobility or cannot travel to our clinic in Panamaram.

Neurological rehabilitation should begin as soon as possible after a stable neurological event (like a stroke) to take advantage of early neuroplasticity. However, even patients with long-standing conditions can benefit from therapy to manage symptoms.
