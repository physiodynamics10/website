About Neurological Physiotherapy

Neurological physiotherapy focuses on the treatment and rehabilitation of individuals with conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves. Our specialized neurological physiotherapists in Wayanad are trained to help patients regain function, improve mobility, and enhance quality of life following neurological events or progressive conditions.

Using evidence-based neurorehabilitation techniques, we work with patients to promote neuroplasticity—the brain's ability to reorganize and form new neural connections—to maximize recovery potential.

Neurological Conditions We Treat

  • Stroke (CVA) rehabilitation
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Multiple Sclerosis (MS)
  • Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)
  • Cerebral Palsy
  • Spinal Cord Injuries
  • Peripheral Neuropathy
  • Guillain-Barré Syndrome
  • Balance and vestibular disorders
  • Ataxia
  • Muscular Dystrophy
  • Post-polio syndrome

Our Neurorehabilitation Approach

Gait & Balance Training

Specialized training to improve walking ability, balance, and reduce fall risk through task-specific practice and assistive devices.

Neurodevelopmental Techniques

Bobath, Proprioceptive Neuromuscular Facilitation (PNF), and other techniques to facilitate normal movement patterns.

Functional Strength Training

Targeted exercises to improve muscle strength, coordination, and functional abilities for daily activities.

Mobility & Transfer Training

Training in safe mobility techniques, wheelchair skills, and transfers to maximize independence.

What to Expect During Your Neurorehabilitation

  1. Comprehensive Neurological Assessment: Detailed evaluation of movement, balance, coordination, strength, and functional abilities
  2. Goal Setting: Collaborative development of realistic, meaningful rehabilitation goals
  3. Personalized Treatment Plan: Customized neurorehabilitation program based on specific neurological condition and goals
  4. Task-Specific Training: Practice of functional activities relevant to daily life
  5. Family/Caregiver Education: Training for family members to support rehabilitation at home
  6. Progress Monitoring: Regular reassessment and adjustment of treatment plan based on progress

Treatment Goals & Benefits

  • Improve mobility and walking
  • Enhance balance and coordination
  • Reduce muscle spasticity
  • Increase strength and endurance
  • Maximize independence in daily activities
  • Prevent secondary complications
  • Improve quality of life

Who Can Benefit

  • Stroke survivors
  • People with Parkinson's disease
  • Multiple sclerosis patients
  • Spinal cord injury patients
  • Traumatic brain injury survivors
  • Individuals with balance disorders
  • Neuropathy patients
