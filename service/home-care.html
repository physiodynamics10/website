Doorstep Clinical Excellence

Professional care where you feel most comfortable

Traveling to a physiotherapy clinic can be painful and challenging for patients recovering from surgery, stroke survivors, or frail elderly individuals. Our home care physiotherapy brings evidence-based clinical rehabilitation directly into your home.

Practicing transfers, balance, and gait in your everyday living space ensures faster, safer recovery and empowers family members with proper patient handling techniques.

Why choose home physiotherapy?

  • Eliminates travel fatigue, vehicle transfers, and waiting times
  • Tailored functional practice on your actual bed, chairs, and stairs
  • Home safety assessment and fall-hazard identification
  • Portable electrotherapy (TENS/IFT) and rehabilitation gear provided
  • Coverage across Panamaram, Mananthavady, Bathery, Kalpetta & Wayanad

Home care physiotherapy FAQs

Everything you need to know about our home visit services in Wayanad.

Physio Dynamics provides professional home care physiotherapy across Wayanad, including Panamaram, Mananthavady, Sulthan Bathery, Kalpetta, and nearby surrounding regions for post-surgical, elderly, and neurological patients.

Home care physiotherapy brings professional rehabilitation services directly to your home. It is designed for elderly patients, individuals recovering from major surgeries (like hip or knee replacement), stroke survivors, or anyone with mobility challenges.

Please keep medical reports, discharge summaries, and imaging (X-rays/MRIs) accessible. Ensure a clear, quiet space with a comfortable bed or sturdy chair where the physiotherapist can conduct the assessment and exercise safely.

Our physiotherapists bring portable electrotherapy units (TENS/IFT), therapeutic resistance bands, gait aids, measurement instruments, and specialized equipment required for your specific treatment.
