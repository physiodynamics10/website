About Home Care Physiotherapy

Home care physiotherapy brings professional rehabilitation services directly to your home. This service is especially beneficial for patients who have difficulty traveling due to mobility issues, post-surgical conditions, chronic illnesses, or elderly patients who prefer the comfort and convenience of receiving treatment at home.

At Physio Dynamics Wayanad, our certified physiotherapists travel to your location with all necessary equipment to provide comprehensive assessment and treatment. We ensure the same level of professional care as in our clinic, tailored to your home environment.

Who Can Benefit from Home Care?

  • Elderly patients with mobility limitations
  • Post-surgical patients (hip/knee replacements, etc.)
  • Stroke rehabilitation patients
  • Patients with chronic conditions (Arthritis, COPD)
  • Palliative care patients
  • Patients with neurological disorders (Parkinson's, MS)
  • Post-hospital discharge patients
  • Patients with balance and fall risk
  • Busy professionals who can't visit clinic
  • Patients preferring privacy of home treatment

Our Home Care Services

Comprehensive Assessment

Thorough evaluation of your condition, home environment, and specific needs to create a personalized treatment plan.

Rehabilitation Exercises

Customized exercise programs using portable equipment to improve strength, mobility, and function.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques including joint mobilization, soft tissue massage, and manipulation in your home.

Home Safety Assessment

Evaluation of your home environment and recommendations for modifications to prevent falls and improve safety.

Mobility Training

Training for walking aids (walkers, canes), transfer techniques, and safe movement within your home.

Caregiver Education

Training family members or caregivers on proper techniques for assisting with daily activities and exercises.
