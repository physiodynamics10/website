5 Pro-Level Exercises for Joint Stability

1

Proprioception Balance Board

Targeted: Ankle & Knee Stability 5 mins

Why it works: After an injury, the "receptors" in your ligaments become sluggish. Balance training retrains your brain to react quickly to prevent a second injury.

  1. Stand on one leg on a flat floor. Close your eyes.
  2. Try to keep perfectly still for 30 seconds.
  3. Progress to a foam pad or balance board as you improve.
2

Eccentric Hamstring Curls

Prevention of Muscle Tears 6 mins

Science: Most hamstring tears occur while the muscle is lengthening. Eccentric training builds strength during that exact "vulnerable" phase.

  1. Lie on your stomach.
  2. Slowly lift your heel to your butt (the curl).
  3. Slowly, very slowly, lower your foot back to the floor over a 5-second count.
  4. Repeat 10 times.
