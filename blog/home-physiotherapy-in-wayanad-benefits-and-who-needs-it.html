Why Choose Home-Visit Physiotherapy?

While clinics have specialized fixed equipment, home therapy offers several unique advantages that a clinic cannot provide:

  • Safe Transition: Post-surgical patients are at risk of injury during travel; home care eliminates this risk.
  • Family Involvement: Your therapist can teach your family how to assist you correctly.
  • Home Assessment: We check your furniture, stairs, and bathroom to identify potential fall hazards.
  • Reduced Stress: Many patients feel more relaxed and focused when therapy happens in their own space.

Who Benefits Most from Home Care?

1

Senior Citizens (Geriatric Care)

Our elders in Wayanad often face challenges with balance and bone density. Home visits focus on "aging in place"—making the home safe while building the strength needed for independent living.

2

Post-Op Recovery (Total Knee/Hip Replacement)

Moving a fresh surgical wound in a bumpy vehicle to reach a clinic can be painful. Home visits ensure your recovery starts the very day you return from the hospital.

3

Neurological Patients (Stroke/Parkinson's)

For patients with balance issues, practicing movements in their own hallway or kitchen is more meaningful than a clinic setting.
