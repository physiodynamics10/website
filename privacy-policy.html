Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to Physio Dynamics. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services in Wayanad, Kerala.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification information (Name, Email address, Phone number, etc.) when you book an appointment, fill out a contact form, or communicate with us directly. We may also collect medical history and treatment records strictly for the purpose of providing physiotherapy services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our physiotherapy services, manage appointments, communicate with you regarding your treatment, and send administrative information.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal and medical information. Your medical records are kept strictly confidential.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may use third-party service providers to help us operate our business (such as online booking systems), provided those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at physiodynamics10@gmail.com.
