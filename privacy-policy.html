Introduction and Purpose

Physio Dynamics – Physiotherapy and Sports Rehabilitation Centre ("Physio Dynamics", "we", "our", or "the clinic") operates the website https://www.physio-dynamics.com/. We are an outpatient physiotherapy and physical rehabilitation facility located at UB City Centre Building, Near Crescent Public School, Panamaram, Wayanad, Kerala, India – 670721.

This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent approach to collecting, utilizing, storing, and safeguarding information gathered from patients, website visitors, and individuals inquiring about our physiotherapy services. As a professional healthcare provider, we respect your confidentiality and adhere to applicable Indian information technology and digital personal data protection principles.

Core Commitment to Patient Privacy

We treat all patient communications with the highest professional discretion. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal contact details with third-party marketers under any circumstance.

2

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information necessary to facilitate communication, evaluate appointment requests, and ensure our website functions reliably. Information collected falls into two primary categories:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Details: Your full name, telephone or mobile phone number, and email address submitted when filling out our contact form, requesting an appointment, or calling our clinic.
  • Appointment Enquiries: Preferred consultation dates, preferred time slots, clinic visit preference versus home care request in Wayanad, and communication notes.
  • Voluntary Health-Related Descriptions: Brief, voluntary descriptions of your clinical concerns (e.g., knee pain, post-fracture rehabilitation, sports injury, or neck stiffness) provided in enquiry messages to help us direct you to the appropriate therapist.
Important Clarification on Medical Records

Our website does not collect, process, or store electronic medical records (EMR), detailed hospital charts, diagnostic imaging files, or financial/payment data. Full clinical assessments, medical history disclosures, and treatment documentation are conducted confidentially and in-person at our clinic in Panamaram.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device & Browser Information: Browser type and version, device model, operating system, screen dimensions, and language preferences.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Pages viewed, time spent on specific educational articles, referral sources (e.g., Google search), and button interactions (such as phone call or booking clicks).
  • Network Data: Truncated/anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses collected solely for regional diagnostics, bot prevention, and aggregated traffic measurement.
3

How Information Is Collected

Information is collected through the following transparent mechanisms:

  • Direct Visitor Submission: When you submit our website contact form, send an email to physiodynamics10@gmail.com, call our clinic at +91 62829 29104, or initiate a chat via WhatsApp.
  • External Booking Portals: When you choose to book an appointment through the Kerala Association of Physiotherapists Co-operative (KAPC) portal linked from our website.
  • Automated Analytics & Cookies: When you browse our pages, provided you have granted consent for analytics storage via our cookie consent banner.
4

How We Use Personal Information

We use the personal information collected through our website strictly for legitimate clinical, administrative, and service improvement purposes:

  • Appointment Coordination: Scheduling, confirming, rescheduling, and answering inquiries regarding clinic consultations and home-care physiotherapy visits across Wayanad.
  • Patient Support: Answering questions regarding our specialized therapies (orthopedic, sports rehabilitation, neurological, pediatric, and chest physiotherapy).
  • Website Administration & Security: Ensuring our digital pages load quickly, detecting broken links, preventing spam submissions, and safeguarding server integrity.
  • Aggregated Understanding: Analyzing general traffic trends to identify which clinical topics and educational guides are most beneficial to our community.
5

Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) (Measurement ID: G-PF3YJYKC6V) orchestrated via Google Tag Manager (GTM) (Container ID: GTM-WKMWL3T3) to assess website usage patterns and improve user experience.

GA4 uses first-party cookies to measure non-personally identifiable traffic metrics, such as page views, scroll depth, and interaction counts. IP addresses are automatically truncated and anonymized by Google before storage.

Google Consent Mode v2 Integration

Our website strictly implements Google Consent Mode v2. On every page, analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data, and ad_personalization are initialized in a denied state by default. No analytics data or cookies are transmitted to Google until you explicitly choose "Accept All" or enable analytics cookies in your Cookie Settings.

6

Microsoft Clarity Analytics

Where activated, our website utilizes Microsoft Clarity to analyze behavioral metrics, visual heatmaps, and session navigation dynamics. This service helps us understand how visitors navigate through our clinical service pages and locate contact details, allowing us to enhance user interface clarity.

Clarity records session interactions without capturing sensitive personal data. Standard data masking is active to prevent any text typed into form input fields from being recorded. Like Google Analytics, Clarity operates in alignment with visitor cookie consent choices.

7

Cookie Usage and Consent Management

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to ensure website reliability and measure traffic. Below is an overview of cookies utilized on our website:

Cookie Category Purpose Default Status Management Option
Strictly Necessary Preserves core website layout, security, and remembers your cookie consent selection (pd_consent_status). Always Active Required for basic site functionality.
Analytics & Performance Measures page visits, bounce rates, and navigation paths (GA4: _ga, _ga_*; Clarity). Denied by Default Can be enabled or disabled via Cookie Settings.
Marketing / Third-Party Controls third-party behavioral tracking or external advertising signals. Denied by Default Can be enabled or disabled via Cookie Settings.

Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You can update, modify, or revoke your cookie consent settings at any time with a single click. Your choices are honored immediately.

Cookie Settings

You may also configure your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to block or delete cookies entirely. Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the visual display of certain website elements.

Third-Party Services and External Links

To provide convenient booking and communication, our website links to or integrates specific third-party tools:

  • KAPC Online Booking System: Appointment links direct visitors to the Kerala Association of Physiotherapists Co-operative portal at kapcwayanad.in. Data entered on that external portal is governed by KAPC's privacy terms.
  • WhatsApp Messaging: When you click our WhatsApp links (+91 62829 29104), conversation data is encrypted and managed subject to WhatsApp’s privacy policy.
  • Google Maps: We provide an embedded Google Map to help patients locate our clinic in Panamaram. Interaction with the map is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Tidio Live Chat: Where active, our chat widget loads asynchronously on user interaction to facilitate real-time enquiry assistance.
9

Data Storage, Retention, and Security Practices

We implement sensible administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate for a healthcare clinic website:

  • Secure Transmission: Our website operates under Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS) with Transport Layer Security (TLS) certificates to protect data in transit between your browser and our server.
  • Restricted Staff Access: Enquiries submitted via email or telephone are accessed strictly by licensed clinical practitioners and authorized clinic administrative personnel who are bound by professional confidentiality.
  • Prudent Data Retention: General website enquiries that do not result in clinical consultations are periodically reviewed and purged. If you become a clinic patient, your clinical records are maintained under Indian clinical record-keeping standards at our physical clinic.
Internet Transmission Disclaimer

While we apply industry-standard security measures, no electronic data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed as 100% invulnerable. We advise visitors not to transmit highly sensitive financial documents or unsolicited medical diagnostic scans through generic web contact forms.

10

Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Physio Dynamics does not sell, lease, trade, or distribute personal contact information to commercial advertising networks or data brokers. Personal data is disclosed only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Operational Service Providers: Trusted IT, hosting, and communication providers who assist in website hosting, domain infrastructure, or email delivery under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal and Statutory Compliance: Where required by applicable Indian law, government regulatory authorities, or a valid order of a court of competent jurisdiction.
  • Protection of Health and Safety: In urgent medical circumstances where disclosure is necessary to protect the immediate physical safety or health of a patient or others, in accordance with medical ethics.
11

Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable Indian data protection laws, visitors and patients enjoy clear rights regarding their personal information:

  • Access and Verification: You may request confirmation of whether we hold contact details you previously submitted through our website.
  • Correction and Rectification: You may ask us to update or correct inaccurate or incomplete contact numbers, spellings, or email addresses.
  • Consent Revocation: You can modify or withdraw your analytics cookie consent at any time using the on-site Cookie Settings modal.
  • Enquiry Data Deletion: You may request the deletion of non-clinical enquiry messages submitted via our online contact form by emailing physiodynamics10@gmail.com.
12

Children's Privacy

Physio Dynamics offers specialized pediatric physiotherapy services for children and adolescents experiencing developmental delays, neurological conditions, or post-injury rehabilitation. However, our website is intended for use by adults, parents, and legal guardians.

We do not knowingly collect personal contact details directly from unsupervised minors under 18 years of age through our website. All clinical enquiries, appointments, and consent for pediatric patients must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian.

External Website Links

Our website may contain hyperlinks to external sites, including medical associations, clinical references, social media channels, or the KAPC online booking portal. These external websites are operated independently of Physio Dynamics and maintain their own distinct privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy notices of any external site you visit. Physio Dynamics assumes no responsibility or liability for the content, privacy practices, or operational procedures of third-party platforms.
