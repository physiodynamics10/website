Slip Disc Treatment Without Surgery: Physiotherapy Options in Wayanad

Sandra Thomas (BPT, HCPC UK) Published: July 24, 2026 6 min read Spine Health & Orthopedics

Experiencing sharp lower back pain radiating down your leg (sciatica)? A herniated or "slip disc" is one of the most common causes of back discomfort. The reassuring news is that up to 90% of slip disc cases can be resolved without surgery through evidence-based conservative physiotherapy.

Understanding a Slip Disc & Sciatica

Intervertebral discs act as shock-absorbing cushions between the bones of your spine. When the tough outer ring (annulus fibrosus) weakens, the gel-like center (nucleus pulposus) can bulge outward, compressing spinal nerves. This nerve compression triggers sharp shooting pain, numbness, or weakness—commonly referred to as sciatica.

Why Surgery is Rarely the First Choice

Unless you experience red-flag symptoms such as loss of bowel or bladder control (cauda equina syndrome) or progressive muscle paralysis, clinical guidelines strongly recommend exhausting conservative treatment for 6 to 12 weeks before considering spinal surgery.
