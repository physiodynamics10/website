Emergency Medical Disclaimer

Physio Dynamics is an outpatient physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation facility. We do not provide acute emergency trauma resuscitation, emergency surgical interventions, or 24/7 hospital critical care.

In Case of Medical Emergency

If you or someone in your care is experiencing acute chest pain, severe breathing distress, sudden weakness or paralysis, acute loss of sensation or bowel/bladder control, suspected compound bone fractures, or head/spinal trauma, do not rely on this website or wait for an appointment enquiry response.

Immediately contact the National Emergency Ambulance Service in India by calling 108 or 112, or report immediately to the nearest hospital casualty/emergency department.

Physiotherapy and Clinical Rehabilitation Services

All clinical treatment provided by Physio Dynamics is subject to professional healthcare standards:

  • In-Person Clinical Assessment: Prior to initiating any therapy, every patient undergoes a thorough baseline evaluation, including movement screening, range of motion measurement, posture assessment, and relevant medical history review.
  • Informed Consent: Our physiotherapists explain proposed rehabilitation techniques, therapeutic modalities (such as manual therapy, joint mobilization, electrotherapy, therapeutic ultrasound, dry needling, cupping, or IASTM where appropriate), expected sensations, and safety considerations. Patients retain the right to ask questions or decline any technique.
  • Home Care Services: Home visits are provided across Wayanad for elderly, post-surgical, or mobility-impaired patients following initial clinical eligibility and geographic confirmation.

Appointment Cancellations, Rescheduling, and Availability

Clinical time is dedicated exclusively to each patient. To ensure fairness and optimal access for individuals in acute physical pain:

  • Advance Courtesy Notice: If you need to cancel or reschedule your consultation, we kindly request a minimum of 24 hours of advance notice by telephone or WhatsApp.

  • Fair Policy: We do not impose arbitrary financial cancellation penalties; we rely on mutual respect for our physiotherapists' time and fellow patients' healthcare needs.
  • Clinic Schedule Adjustments: While we strive to adhere strictly to booked times, emergency patient presentations or complex clinical needs may occasionally require slight scheduling adjustments. We thank you for your understanding.

Patient Responsibilities

Safe, effective physical rehabilitation is a collaborative partnership between patient and therapist. As a patient, you agree to:

  • Provide Truthful Health Information: Fully disclose your complete medical background, past surgical interventions, implanted cardiac pacemakers, metal hardware/implants, cardiovascular conditions, hypertension, diabetes, osteoporosis, active infections, medications, and pregnancy during your clinical assessment.
  • Report Discomfort Immediately: Communicate openly with your physiotherapist during treatment regarding any unusual pain, sharp sensations, dizziness, nausea, or discomfort.
  • Adhere to Clinical Guidance: Carefully follow prescribed home exercise routines, posture modifications, and safety instructions provided by your therapist. Do not attempt unapproved strenuous activities that contradict clinical guidance.
  • Notify of Clinical Changes: Inform your therapist of any changes in your symptoms, new diagnostic reports (X-rays, MRIs), or medications prescribed by other treating physicians.

Treatment Outcomes and Recovery Expectations

Physiotherapy is an evidence-based medical science dedicated to restoring functional mobility, managing pain, and enhancing physical performance.

No Guarantee of Specific Clinical Results

Human anatomy, tissue pathology, cellular healing rates, chronicity of condition, and individual biomechanical factors vary substantially between patients. While Physio Dynamics utilizes modern, evidence-backed clinical protocols, we cannot and do not guarantee complete pain elimination, identical recovery timelines, or specific clinical results for any individual patient.

Clinical progress depends heavily on accurate diagnostic evaluation, timely attendance of scheduled sessions, adherence to prescribed home exercise regimens, ergonomic modifications, and general health status.
