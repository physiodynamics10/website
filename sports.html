What is Sports Physiotherapy?

Sports physiotherapy focuses on the prevention, assessment, and rehabilitation of sports-related injuries. It helps improve mobility, strength, endurance, and ensures a safe return to sport.

Who Can Benefit from Sports Physiotherapy?

  • Ligament, muscle, and tendon injuries
  • Gym and fitness-related pain
  • Post-surgical sports rehabilitation
  • Chronic joint or muscle pain
  • Return-to-sport conditioning
