Services

We provides

Orthopedic Physiotherapy

Orthopedic Physiotherapy focuses on diagnosing, managing, and treating conditions related to the bones, joints, ligaments, and muscles. It helps restore mobility, reduce pain, and improve strength after injuries, surgeries, or chronic orthopedic conditions.

Sports Physiotherapy

Sports Physiotherapy is dedicated to the prevention, assessment, and treatment of sports-related injuries. It helps athletes of all levels recover faster, improve performance, and reduce the risk of future injuries through tailored rehabilitation and training programs.

Neurological Physiotherapy

Neurological Physiotherapy focuses on treating patients with disorders of the nervous system such as stroke, Parkinson's disease, spinal cord injuries, and multiple sclerosis. It aims to improve mobility, coordination, balance, and overall functional independence.

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Pediatric Physiotherapy specializes in the treatment of infants, children, and adolescents with physical challenges. It helps improve movement, strength, posture, and motor skills to support healthy growth and development.

Chest Physiotherapy

Chest Physiotherapy helps clear mucus from the lungs and improve breathing in patients with respiratory conditions such as asthma, bronchitis, pneumonia, or COPD. It includes techniques like percussion, vibration, and breathing exercises to enhance lung function.

Home Care

Home Care Physiotherapy provides personalized treatment in the comfort of your home for patients who are elderly, post-surgical, or unable to visit the clinic. It ensures continuity of care, convenience, and faster recovery in a familiar environment.
