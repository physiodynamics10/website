About Us

Expert Care in Wayanad

Physio Dynamics is Wayanad's premier physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation centre, located in Panamaram near Crescent Public School. Since August 2024, we have helped patients recover from knee pain, shoulder injuries, slip disc, sciatica, post-fracture stiffness, and neurological conditions like stroke and Parkinson's disease.

Our qualified physiotherapists use evidence-based techniques to deliver lasting results. Every patient receives a full assessment and a personalized treatment plan designed around their lifestyle, recovery goals, and condition severity.
